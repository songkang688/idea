# SEG-0276-M — Per-Category Prior Shape Field (Stage 5, Windows1)

- **ID:** SEG-0276-M | **Class:** Mechanistic/behavioral-law | **Risk (R2):** HRHR, killer is inference-only
- **Status:** Stage-5 complete; recommendation at end. Written as a late re-admission (wrongly omitted at Stage-4 cut).
- **One-line claim:** Segmentation-VLM masks are not pure readouts of local evidence; they interpolate between instance evidence and a *category-canonical shape field*, with interpolation weight α that (a) rises as local evidence degrades, (b) rises with prompt semantic strength, and (c) has a coefficient structure that transfers across model families.
- **Positioning:** Geometry-level. HalluSegBench (arXiv 2506.21546) asks *whether* a model segments something that isn't there and how large/where the spurious footprint is (presence-level: ΔIoU, CMS, CCMS). SEG-0276-M asks how the *geometry of a mask for a genuinely present object* deforms toward class-canonical shape as a continuous function of evidence and language. No existing benchmark measures that.

---

## 1. Research questions

- **RQ1 (existence):** When local visual evidence for an object is degraded while the prompt is held fixed, does the predicted mask drift *specifically toward the category-canonical shape*, beyond generic smoothing/regularization?
- **RQ2 (language causality):** At matched evidence, does stronger prompt semantics (box-only → class name → referring → rich description) increase the drift? Does a *wrong-class* prompt pull the mask toward the wrong class's canonical shape?
- **RQ3 (law + transfer):** Is the drift captured by a low-dimensional interpolation law α(e, s), and do its coefficients (or per-category attraction profiles) transfer across SAM-class, Grounded-SAM-class, and LISA-class models?

## 2. Falsifiable hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Quantitative pass bar | Falsified if |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | α increases monotonically with evidence degradation dose e | Spearman ρ(α, e) > 0 in ≥ 70% of categories; median α at max dose ≥ 0.25 for text-prompted models | α flat or non-monotone in most categories |
| H2 | α increases with prompt semantic strength s at matched e | Δα = α(s=rich) − α(s=box-only) ≥ 0.10, bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 | Δα ≈ 0 (drift identical for box-only SAM and rich-text LISA) |
| H3 | Attraction is semantic, not generic | SSA > 0 (§5 controls): α toward correct-class prior exceeds α toward wrong-class prior, global-mean prior, and matched morphological smoothing | Correct-class direction not preferred → phenomenon is regularization, idea dead |
| H4 | Transfer | Per-category α-profiles across model pairs: Spearman ρ ≥ 0.6; slope ratio β_s/β_e agrees within ×2 across models | Profiles uncorrelated → "law" is model-specific curve fitting |

H0 (the world where we kill it): degraded evidence causes shrinkage/smoothing that projects incidentally onto the prior direction; language changes presence decisions (HalluSegBench regime) but not geometry.

## 3. Interpolation model — math for α

**Canonical frame.** For each instance i with GT mask M_i^gt: translate by GT centroid, scale by √area(M_i^gt), rotate by GT second-moment major axis. The *same* GT-derived transform is applied to the predicted mask M̂_i (prediction-independent alignment; no circularity through the model under test).

**Shape embedding.** φ(M) = signed distance function of M on a 128×128 canonical grid, clipped to ±τ (τ = 0.25 frame width), flattened to ℝ^d. SDF-space linear interpolation is the classical statistical-shape-model setting (Leventon–Grimson–Faugeras, CVPR 2000), so the interpolation model has a principled geometric meaning.

**Prior shape field.** For category c: Φ̄_c = mean of φ over N_c ≥ 200 *human-annotated* training masks, plus PCA basis U_c ∈ ℝ^{d×K} (K ≈ 20, ≥ 90% variance). For viewpoint-dependent categories (car, chair), k-means (k = 4–8) in SDF space and use nearest-cluster mean as Φ̄_c(i); report pooled and per-cluster results.

**Per-instance interpolation coefficient (closed form).**

    α̂_i = ⟨ φ(M̂_i) − φ(M_i^gt), Φ̄_c − φ(M_i^gt) ⟩ / ‖ Φ̄_c − φ(M_i^gt) ‖²

i.e., the scalar projection of the prediction's deviation from instance truth onto the GT→prior line. Sanity anchors: α̂ = 0 if prediction equals GT, α̂ = 1 if it equals the class prior.

**Model-adequacy check (this is what makes the interpolation *model* falsifiable, not just α).** Residual r_i = φ(M̂_i) − [(1−α̂_i)φ(M_i^gt) + α̂_i Φ̄_c]; orthogonality ratio ρ_i = ‖r_i‖² / ‖φ(M̂_i) − φ(M_i^gt)‖². The 1-D interpolation model is adequate iff median ρ ≤ 0.4 (≥ 60% of deviation energy on the GT→prior line). If ρ is high but the residual lies inside span(U_c), the correct model is *subspace attraction*, not scalar interpolation (→ Pivot 1, §11).

**The law.** With degradation dose e (standardized per-corruption index, plus a model-free evidence proxy: fraction of in-mask gradient energy retained) and prompt-strength rung s ∈ {0..4} (§6):

    α(e, s) = σ( β₀ + β_e·e + β_s·s )        (logistic link; fit per model, per category-group)

Transfer claim = stability of β_s/β_e and of per-category α-profiles across models (H4). α is dimensionless and frame-normalized, which is what makes cross-model comparison legitimate.

## 4. Prior field estimation without circularity

1. **Annotations only, never model outputs.** Φ̄_c is built exclusively from human-drawn masks (LVIS v1 train polygons; higher boundary quality than COCO). No SAM/VLM output ever enters the prior.
2. **Dataset disjointness.** Prior from LVIS train; evaluation instances from COCO val2017 / LVIS val / ADE20K. Overlapping images excluded by COCO image id.
3. **Leave-instance-out** trivially holds under (2); additionally exclude same-image instances.
4. **Annotation-free cross-check (rigid classes).** For ~6 rigid categories, rebuild Φ̄_c from silhouettes of rendered ShapeNet models (uniform viewpoints). If α̂ conclusions agree with annotation-built priors, the result is not an artifact of annotator style.
5. **Annotator-style control.** Recompute α̂ with priors built from COCO-train instead of LVIS-train; report both. Divergence bounds the annotation-style contribution.

## 5. Controls

- **Scrambled text (s=1):** shuffled words / length-matched non-words. Separates language-*content* effects from text-conditioning-pathway effects. If α rises with degradation equally under scrambled text, the prior is visual, not language-injected.
- **Class-name vs referring vs rich (s=2/3/4), fixed model:** text varied *within* one model (LISA-class) so architecture is constant; referring expressions add localization, so s=3 vs s=2 is interpreted only jointly with the box-anchored s=0 rung.
- **Model family sweep:** SAM/SAM2 (box prompt, no language) vs Grounded-SAM (GroundingDINO box + SAM, class-name text) vs LISA-7B/13B, GLaMM, PixelLM, SESAME (VLM-decoded masks). SAM's α curve is the language-free floor.
- **Wrong-class prior direction:** compute α̂ against Φ̄_{c′} of a shape-dissimilar class; **global prior:** Φ̄_all over all classes; **matched smoothing:** morphological opening/closing of the GT at matched boundary-length reduction. Define **SSA_i = α̂(correct) − max{α̂(wrong), α̂(global), α̂(smooth)}**. H3 lives or dies on SSA.
- **Area/scale confound:** canonical frame removes global scale; additionally verify α̂ is not explained by area ratio alone (partial correlation).
- **Presence guard:** instances where the model abstains or IoU(pred, GT) < 0.1 are excluded from α̂ (that is the HalluSegBench presence regime, analyzed separately in §10).

## 6. Protocols

**Evidence-degradation (object-interior only; background and hence referring-expression validity untouched):**

| Axis | Doses | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Blur | σ ∈ {2,4,8,16,32} px @1024 | Gaussian blur inside dilated GT mask |
| Noise/corruption | ImageNet-C severities 1–5 | Hendrycks–Dietterich corruption code, masked |
| Texture replacement | 1 level × 3 styles | AdaIN stylization of mask interior (Geirhos ICLR 2019 protocol); silhouette edge partially preserved |
| Occlusion | {25, 50, 75}% of mask area | random-walk occluder patch, inpainted borders; GT for scoring = original modal mask (amodal drift analyzed as a labeled sub-case) |
| Camouflage/contrast | blend {0.25, 0.5, 0.75} toward local mean color | approximates concealed-scene regime where SAM is known weak (arXiv 2304.06022) |

**Prompt-strength ladder s:** s0 = box prompt, no text (SAM/SAM2); s1 = scrambled text; s2 = bare class name ("dog"); s3 = RefCOCO-style referring expression; s4 = category-attribute-rich description ("the dog — a four-legged animal with pointed ears and a tail"); s5 (reviewer experiment only) = plausible wrong class. Rungs s1–s5 run on text-capable models with all image conditions crossed.

## 7. Datasets (all existing, public)

COCO 2017 (instances), LVIS v1 (priors + eval), ADE20K (out-of-COCO-style eval), RefCOCO/RefCOCO+/RefCOCOg (referring prompts), ReasonSeg (LISA; reasoning prompts), HalluSegBench / HuggingFace `PLAN-Lab/Hallu` (counterfactual pairs for §10), FP-RefCOCO(+/g) (SESAME; false-premise prompts), gRefCOCO (no-target expressions), COD10K + CAMO (naturally evidence-poor camouflage — free "high-dose" condition), OCHuman and COCOA (occlusion / amodal GT to separate canonical-shape drift from amodal completion). Degradations are transformations of COCO/LVIS images with released corruption/stylization code — no new dataset is invented.

## 8. Metrics

- **Shape space:** α̂ (SDF projection, §3); orthogonality ratio ρ; projection energy onto class PCA subspace U_c; PCA-coefficient trajectories of predictions as e increases.
- **Contour space:** Chamfer distance from predicted contour to class-mean contour vs to instance-GT contour; report Δd(e,s) = d(pred, prior) − d(pred, GT) (should decrease under H1); Hausdorff and boundary-F at 2 px tolerance as standard quality context.
- **Prior-attraction index:** PAI = [d(pred_degraded, prior) − d(pred_clean, prior)] / d(pred_clean, prior) (negative = attraction), robust to per-category chamfer scale.
- **Semantic specificity:** SSA (§5).
- **Law fit:** McFadden pseudo-R² of the logistic α(e,s) fit; **transfer:** cross-model Spearman ρ of per-category α-profiles; β_s/β_e ratio table.

## 9. Killer experiment (3 days, inference-only, 1×A100-80GB)

- **Setup:** 20 categories (10 canonical-rigid: stop sign, clock, sports ball, frisbee, banana, donut, pizza, skateboard, bottle, guitar; 10 deformable/viewpoint: person, cat, dog, bird, horse, car, chair, umbrella, teddy bear, handbag), ~100 instances each (COCO val + LVIS val), area ≥ 32² px.
- **Day 1 (CPU-heavy):** build Φ̄_c/U_c from LVIS train; implement SDF/α̂ pipeline; verify anchors α̂(GT)=0, α̂(prior)=1; compute smoothing-control baseline; generate blur×5, occlusion×3, camouflage×1 ladders (~18k degraded images).
- **Day 2 (GPU):** sweep SAM (box), SAM2 (box), Grounded-SAM (s1, s2), LISA-7B (s1–s4). ≈ 2k instances × 9 image conditions × ~6 model×prompt cells ≈ 100k masks; LISA-7B ≈ 1 s/sample → ~10 GPU-h; SAM/Grounded-SAM ≪ that. Fits in one day with batching.
- **Day 3:** fit α̂, ρ, SSA, logistic law; run the two mini reviewer probes (§10) on 200 instances / 300 HalluSegBench pairs; decide against gates.
- **Continue gates (all four):** H1 monotonicity in ≥ 70% categories; H2 Δα ≥ 0.10 with CI > 0; H3 median SSA > 0 with CI > 0; median ρ ≤ 0.5.

## 10. Reviewer shut-up experiment

Two probes that jointly rebut "it's just blur-induced smoothing" and "this is HalluSegBench re-run":

1. **Wrong-class prompt injection (causal, geometry-level).** At high degradation, prompt LISA/Grounded-SAM with a plausible wrong class (s5: "cat" on a dog). Prediction: α̂ measured *against the prompted class's prior* increases with degradation dose while α̂ against the true class's prior falls — language causally selects *which* shape field attracts the mask. Generic smoothing cannot produce prompt-dependent attraction direction.
2. **Shape of hallucinations (bridge to presence-level).** On HalluSegBench counterfactual images where models wrongly segment a removed object, test whether the hallucinated mask's shape matches the queried category's prior field above a permutation-control chance level (match to shuffled-category priors). Positive result: presence-level hallucination and geometry-level attraction are the same underlying prior, at two doses — a claim neither HalluSegBench nor the texture-bias papers makes.

## 11. Stop / kill / pivot

- **KILL** if SSA ≤ 0 across category groups *and* wrong-class injection shows no prompted-class attraction: drift is generic regularization. Salvage: 1-page negative result ("mask geometry is evidence-driven; hallucination is presence-only"), fold into the hallucination-analysis writeup; redirect compute to SEG-0085-M.
- **PIVOT 1** if median ρ > 0.5 but residuals concentrate in span(U_c): replace scalar α with subspace-attraction energy; protocol and data unchanged, math section rewritten. Same paper skeleton, weaker but still novel claim.
- **PIVOT 2** if the effect appears only as whole-mask hallucination (presence flips) rather than continuous deformation: merge with probe §10.2 into a short "the shape of segmentation hallucinations" paper targeting the HalluSegBench audience.
- **STOP-rule for scale-up:** do not spend past 5 GPU-days unless H2 *and* H3 both pass at Day 3.

## 12. Compute budget (inference-only throughout)

Killer: ≤ 3 A100-days (incl. reruns). Full paper: killer + GLaMM/PixelLM/SESAME/LISA-13B sweeps, ADE20K/COD10K/CAMO/OCHuman extensions, ShapeNet prior cross-check, seeds: ≈ 15–20 A100-days. Zero training. Optional mechanistic add-on (attention-to-object-interior vs α correlation): +3–5.

## 13. Expected curves (predictions, not measurements)

- **α vs log-dose:** sigmoid; SAM box-only floor rising to ~0.1–0.2 at max dose (visual prior only); LISA s4 rising to ~0.4–0.7; Grounded-SAM s2 between; scrambled text ≈ box-only floor for Grounded-SAM, slightly above floor for LISA (text pathway leakage).
- **Δα(s) at fixed high dose:** monotone ladder s0 < s1 ≈ s0 < s2 < s3 ≤ s4, biggest jump s1→s2 (content, not conditioning).
- **Category structure:** canonical-rigid classes (stop sign, clock) show larger, cleaner α than deformable classes; per-category α-profiles rank-correlate ρ ≈ 0.6–0.8 across LISA-class models, lower (~0.4–0.6) between SAM-class and LISA-class.
- **Wrong-class injection:** crossing curves — attraction toward prompted-class prior overtakes true-class prior beyond a dose threshold.

## 14. Negative-result interpretation

If H3 fails: segmentation-VLM geometry is evidence-driven and hallucination is a presence phenomenon — a genuine boundary result that says HalluSegBench-style abstention training (RobustSeg/CFT) addresses the whole problem; publishable only as a section/workshop note, not a paper. If H1/H3 pass but H4 fails: still a per-model finding ("LISA-class models carry language-selectable shape priors") without the transfer law — mid-tier paper. If only presence-level effects exist: Pivot 2. In every branch the spend to knowledge is ≤ 3 GPU-days.

## 15. ROI vs Constrained SAM-FT and vs SEG-0085-M

- **vs Constrained SAM-FT (method/fine-tuning idea):** SAM-FT needs training compute (tens of GPU-days per ablation row) before its first informative result, and its territory is already occupied: TextureSAM (WACV 2026) does controlled texture-replacement fine-tuning of SAM-2, and RobustSeg (HalluSegBench paper) does counterfactual fine-tuning for abstention. Its upside is an incremental method delta in a crowded lane. SEG-0276-M reaches a kill/continue decision in ≤ 3 inference-only GPU-days and, if it survives, yields a *law + transferable coefficient* — a claim type (quantitative science of segmentation VLMs) with no incumbent. Strictly better expected-information-per-GPU-day; also, if 0276-M survives, its α measurement becomes the natural *evaluation* for any future constrained-FT method, so the ideas compose rather than compete.
- **vs SEG-0085-M (fellow mechanistic finalist):** complementary rather than substitutive — 0276-M is a behavioral input–output law needing no internals access or training, so it diversifies the portfolio against the failure mode where internals-dependent analysis stalls; shared infrastructure (model zoo inference harness, COCO/LVIS tooling) amortizes across both. It should not displace 0085-M; the two together cover mechanism and behavior of the same prior-override phenomenon.

**Honest risk ledger:** (a) HRHR — H0 (generic smoothing) is genuinely plausible; mitigated by SSA controls and the cheap kill. (b) Novelty adjacency: SAM cue-bias papers (arXiv 2311.11465 texture-biased; TextureSAM finding SAM-2 shape-biased — the literature is unsettled, which 0276-M's continuous α would adjudicate); amodal completion (COCOA, pix2gestalt) covers *occlusion-conditioned* shape completion — 0276-M must, and does, treat occlusion as one dose axis among five and claim the *language-modulated interpolation law*, not shape completion per se. (c) Referring-expression rung confounds localization with semantics; handled by within-model text-only contrasts plus the box-anchored floor, and stated as a scoped limitation.

## 16. Recommendation: include / exclude from final 3

**INCLUDE — conditionally, in place of Constrained SAM-FT, not in place of SEG-0085-M.** Concretely: run the Day-1–3 killer immediately (≤ 3 A100-days, inference-only, no dependency on the other two ideas' pipelines). If all four Day-3 gates pass, 0276-M takes the third slot: it is the only candidate offering a falsifiable quantitative law with a built-in causal probe (wrong-class injection) and a presence↔geometry bridge to an existing benchmark, at near-zero compute risk. If any gate fails, revert the slot to Constrained SAM-FT and keep the §10.2 hallucination-shape probe as a section-level contribution elsewhere. The original Stage-4 omission was a mistake precisely because the R2 rating ("killer can be inference-only") makes the option value of this idea almost free to price.
