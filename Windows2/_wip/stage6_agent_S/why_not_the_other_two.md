# Why Not the Other Two (Agent S, Top 5 → Final 3)

The two seats of Q's Top 5 that do not receive Stage-7 packages. Elimination is not death: both remain in

## B-0194 (+B-0169) — segmentation sycophancy under randomized prompt dose — OUT (2 votes of 8)

**What it is and what it isn't:** The best-engineered protocol in the surviving set — randomized dose
assignment kills selection bias structurally, and the RClicks-hostage tripwire (if click-policy variance
dominates dose effects within strata, both headlines are declared artifacts and the program stops) is the
best-formed stop criterion the pipeline produced. Nothing in this cut disputes any of that. It lost on
portfolio arithmetic, not on merit.

**The three reasons it lost seat 3 (Round 6, 5–2–1):**

1. **Correlation with A-0028.** A-0028 is binding-protected; seating B-0194 makes two of the three
   Stage-7 packages SAM-family behavioral probes — concentrated exposure to one model family's relevance,
   one reviewer pool, and one shared rebuttal ("probing a model you didn't train"). At 3 seats,
   decorrelation is worth more than at 5.
2. **No clock vs a closing clock.** B-0194's niche is verifiably slow — Q's record: the SAM-UQ literature
   cites the phenomenon but nobody owns the randomized dose-response law; no scoop clock beyond routine
   re-search. B-0114's register-free-backbone window closes every quarter. A bet that keeps intact should
   not evict a bet that expires.
3. **Ceiling.** M-H, the lowest in the 5, against B-0114's H — and at 3 seats the R2 reservation
   (all-deflationary-branches → no flagship) binds harder than at 5.

**MED's swap motion (A-0331 in its place) also failed** on the binding's own conditions: A-0331's
silencing experiment (~2 GPU-weeks instrumented training) is not cheaper than B-0194's, and J's
"pursue first or drop" cannot be honored third in the execution queue (see `battle.md` Round 5 Part C).

**Named status — FIRST REPLACEMENT SEAT (SEG's dissent, banked):** If any of the final 3 dies at its
gate — C-0306's blind spot immaterial everywhere, A-0028's placebo arm invalidating the probe, or
B-0114's exp_02/4-arm outcome landing in the kill cell — **B-0194 activates without a new battle.** It is
battle-ready, fully specified (O's battle-locked design; ~2 GPU-weeks; no prerequisite), and its scope
note (claims restricted to the tested click-policy envelope; medical click behavior out of scope) travels
with it. Off-portfolio start is NOT recommended for B-0194 (unlike C-0338's gate): its cost is the full
2-GPU-week protocol, which is exactly the spend the package decision exists to authorize.

**Standing dissent carried (SEG + MED):** with B-0194 out, the Top-3 has zero safety/interactive coverage;
the PI co-signs that hole by accepting this cut.

## C-0338 (+C-0007 arm) — the size-resolved human ceiling — OUT (1 vote of 8)

**What it is and what it isn't:** The cheapest H-payoff item in the entire pipeline (1-day LVIS
double-annotation census gate + 1–2 GPU-days) and the portfolio's best silencing experiment — the
pre-registered per-size-bin crossing point makes "AP-small progress is meaningful" unstateable below the
measured bin. Nothing in this cut disputes its merit either. It lost on package scarcity and genre
arithmetic.

**The two reasons it lost seat 3 (Round 6, 5–2–1):**

1. **Package scarcity (R2's argument, the decisive one).** A Stage-7 package is 28 sections of program
   infrastructure; C-0338 resolves for ≈2 GPU-days. A project that resolves for the price of a long
   weekend needs a calendar slot, not a package. Seating it would have spent the scarcest resource in the
   pipeline on the least package-hungry item — and evicted the perishable H-ceiling mechanism bet to do it.
2. **Audit-genre / AC-risk arithmetic.** The PI lean named the collision: C-0338 is the audit genre
   ("your evaluation practice is unfalsifiable below X" — it took the {A-0061, A-0206, C-0338} token in
   Round 3 without protest). C-0306's headline already opens adversarially ("NMS destroys coverage");
   the Top-3 reads constructive/science with zero audit seats and reads "complaint portfolio" with one.
   Structurally closing the AC-risk was worth more than the seat.

**MED's swap motion (A-0074 in its place) also failed** on the binding's own conditions: A-0074's
deployed-pipeline survey is calendar-bound and strictly more expensive than C-0338's 1-day census, and
audit-for-audit does not reduce cap exposure (see `battle.md` Round 5 Part C).

**Adopted 8–0 — the figure is not declined, the package is:** the census gate (N exp_06 step 1: size
distribution of LVIS double-annotated instances) **runs off-portfolio in week one** (1 CPU-day, before any
annotation spend), co-scheduled with the three seated gates. Its full trigger lattice is preserved
verbatim on the record:

- **Census thin** (small-bin coverage too sparse for usable CIs) → the contingent small-strata labeling N
  priced, or the line stops; **C-0057 remains first alternate for the eval genre (8–0, from Round 4)** in
  any future re-cut.
- **Crossing fires material** (model-GT AP exceeds human-human agreement AP in deployed size bins) → the
  PI re-opens with a measured branch in hand; nothing in this cut prejudices a fourth package or a
  next-cycle seat.
- **Crossing below deployed bins** (headline muted) → **A-0206's named re-entry claim triggers** (Round 3
  Part C, reaffirmed unanimously in Round 4) — and the PI's corpus-commissioning decision remains due this
  quarter on its own merits, independent of any seat list.

**Standing dissent carried (HU):** the cheapest H-payoff figure in the pipeline now has no package owner;
if the census fires, the PI will be deciding under time pressure what could have been decided here.
