# Feasibility Census — Public Artifacts for the B ∪ C Portfolio (Agent G)

Date: 2026-08-28. All FOUND URLs below were retrieved via live web search **this session**; nothing is cited from memory alone. Items I could not verify this session are marked PARTIAL (with what was verified) or MISSING. Per mandate: missing → MISSING, no invented URLs.

This census answers Agent D's #1 information request ("cached-prediction availability census … would re-rank the whole re-analysis family") and checks the checkpoints E's top-20 assume.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **No public bundles of per-instance COCO/LVIS predictions exist.** The COCO organizers do not release submissions; there is no standard repository of prediction JSONs. What exists instead is *checkpoints*: Detectron2's zoo (~dozens of COCO models) and MMDetection's zoo (hundreds of configs with weights). Consequence: every C idea sold as "analysis-only on cached predictions" **reprices to "inference + analysis"** — cheap (COCO val2017 is 5k images; 30 models is GPU-days, not GPU-months), but the ROI labels in D's scores were mildly optimistic. No idea dies from this alone.
2. **The double-annotation artifacts D asked about DO exist publicly.** COCO-ReM (train+val refined masks) is on HuggingFace; the LVIS doubly-annotated 5k-image consistency subset is public in processed v1.0 form. This **upgrades the whole GT-epistemology bundle** (C-0046, C-0338, C-0344, C-0311, C-0057, C-0326), exactly the outcome D said "would push the GT-epistemology bundle to the very top of my board."
3. **One new prior-work hit surfaced during the census:** *Label Convergence: Defining an Upper Performance Bound in Object Recognition through Contradictory Annotations* (arXiv 2409.09412) already uses the LVIS doubly-annotated subset + bootstrap to estimate a human-consistency performance bound (62.6–67.5 mAP; Co-DETR at 66.8 near the bound). This partially occupies C-0071 and is mandatory related work for C-0338/C-0046. Handled in `collisions.md` §2.6 (C-0071 demoted to an arm of C-0338).
4. **All VFM checkpoints E's top-20 need are public** (DINOv2 ± registers, CLIP, OpenCLIP, SigLIP/SigLIP2, SAM/SAM2, official VPT code). No feasibility blocker on the B side; the only conditions are compute (B-0307, B-0293 pretraining) — a budget issue, not an artifact issue.
5. **The LVIS long-tail re-analysis family is the real casualty:** it needs checkpoints of *many published long-tail methods*, and only a subset is in maintained zoos (MMDetection ships Seesaw ± RFS variants; most other methods live in per-paper repos of unverified state). The family is capped to two hosts (C-0276, C-0256) with the rest demoted to arms or dropped.

## 3. Re-ranking of C's "analysis-only" family (as Agent D requested)

D's request: "A one-day audit of model zoos would re-rank the whole re-analysis family: if predictions must be regenerated at scale, 'analysis-only' ROI claims deflate."

Verdict: **predictions must be regenerated, but regeneration is cheap for COCO-val-scale audits and only painful where the idea needs *many third-party method* checkpoints.** Specifically:

- **Unchanged rank (regeneration trivial):** C-0309 (+0328), C-0302 bundle, C-0136 (its cost was always training, not caching), C-0326, C-0119, C-0158, C-0306, C-0011, C-0279, C-0122, C-0298, C-0190. These keep their D ranks; ROI notes should read "inference + analysis".
- **Upgraded:** C-0046 (+0080), C-0338, C-0344, C-0311, C-0057 — the double-annotation and COCO-ReM artifacts exist publicly (§2 rows 3–4). The GT-epistemology bundle moves to the top of C's board, as D anticipated.
- **Downgraded (missing/partial caches):** C-0253 → DROP, C-0271 → DROP, C-0254 → DROP (missing deployment-tail artifact), C-0255 → demoted to arm of C-0276, C-0261 → demoted to arm of C-0256, C-0264 → demoted to arm of C-0122. Net: **6 C analysis ideas downgraded** (3 dropped, 3 demoted to hosted arms).
- **New risk found:** Label Convergence (arXiv 2409.09412) partially occupies C-0071 (demoted to arm of C-0338) and is now mandatory related work for C-0338/C-0046/C-0311. This is a census by-product, not a cache issue.
