## 15 near-misses

Ranked by how close the call was. Each would be a defensible keep; the stated reason is why it lost the marginal slot. "Merge flag" means the idea should be folded into the named survivor's experimental plan rather than dropped.

1. **C-0330 — Perturbation noise floors for boundary metrics** (avg 4.23, Risk L). Establishes per-metric noise floors by re-scoring under controlled mask perturbations. Cut only because kept 0321 + 0339 already form a two-paper metrology suite; 0330 is the third chapter of the same paper. **Merge flag → 0321/0339.**
2. **C-0318 — Image-free prior floors for benchmarks** (avg 4.23, Risk L). Trains label-only models to expose how much benchmark performance is dataset prior rather than image evidence. Extremely cheap and damning where positive; cut because it is the canonical *dataset-batch* theme — belongs with the dataset-failure batch. **Merge flag → dataset batch (prior-floor theme).**
3. **C-0317 — 8× downsampled masks: benchmark sensitivity** (avg 4.15, Risk L). Asks whether benchmarks can even detect severe boundary degradation. Same sensitivity-certificate logic as 0339; folding it in costs one extra sweep. **Merge flag → 0339/0321.**
4. **C-0232 — Boundary-error → clinical-endpoint sensitivity map** (avg 4.08, Risk M). Maps which boundary errors actually move clinical endpoints (RT dose, volumetrics). High significance, but kept 0177 runs the general clinical-vs-benchmark divergence program; this is its boundary-specific chapter. **Merge flag → 0177.**
5. **C-0185 — ROI-grid boundary ceiling, regime split** (avg 4.08, Risk L). Derives and tests the boundary-accuracy ceiling implied by ROI-align grid size. Clean, cheap; weakened because learned sub-grid interpolation can beat the naive bound, making the "ceiling" soft rather than hard.
6. **C-0334 — Evaluate at 2× resolution: rankings reshuffle** (avg 4.08, Risk L). One-sweep protocol-stability check; folds into the kept evaluation-integrity suite. **Merge flag → 0339/0321.**
7. **C-0335 — Linear probe matches the fancy decoder** (avg 4.08, Risk L). Deflationary readout claim; served by kept 0301's readout-ceiling program, which asks the same question with a better design.
8. **C-0338 — Zero skip connections at inference** (avg 4.08, Risk L). Startling if true, trivially cheap; cut because inference-time ablation puts the network off-distribution, so a negative result is uninterpretable without retraining controls (which stop it being cheap).
9. **C-0305 — Resample vs reweight = BatchNorm interaction** (avg 4.00, Risk M). Attributes the resample-vs-reweight folklore to normalization statistics. Tidy, mechanistic, likely true; moderate reach since modern backbones increasingly use LayerNorm/GroupNorm.
10. **C-0293 — TTA degrades calibration and boundaries** (avg 4.00, Risk L). Audits the hidden costs of test-time augmentation. Real and useful; kept 0056 studies TTA as a drift process and should absorb these endpoints. **Merge flag → 0056.**
11. **C-0284 — VQ codebook caps boundary precision** (avg 4.00, Risk M). Timely for unified tokenizer-based segmentation; cut because the oracle decoder approximation needed to isolate the codebook's contribution is contentious enough to dominate reviews.
12. **C-0259 — "Within rater variability" safety argument fails** (avg 4.00, Risk M). Audits the field's favorite deployment justification. Important, but the contribution is argumentative rather than experimental; runs best as a companion analysis inside kept 0245.
13. **C-0240 — Physics predicts rater disagreement** (avg 4.00, Risk M). Predicts inter-rater disagreement from acquisition physics (partial volume, contrast). Strong scientific idea; trimmed on the C9 budget because kept 0206 (C8) already tests the physics bound directly on model accuracy — 0240 is its rater-side twin. **Merge flag → 0206.**
14. **C-0098 — Visual prompts = normalization perturbation** (avg 4.00, Risk M). Claims visual prompt tuning reduces to a structured perturbation of normalization statistics. Sharp mechanism split with a clean test; trimmed because C4's kept set already carries two deflationary prompt mechanisms (0091, 0113) and budget favored breadth.
15. **C-0090 — Prompts act as priors, not evidence** (avg 4.00, Risk M). Decomposes prompt influence into prior-shift vs likelihood-shift with an elegant Bayesian read. Cut on cluster budget; the decomposition partially re-derives kept 0091's dataset-statistics account in different language.
