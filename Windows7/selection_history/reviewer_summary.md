# Windows7 — Reviewer Summary

What the reviewers actually changed. Objections that produced no design change are not listed;
objections that were merely rebutted in prose are marked as such, because a rebuttal is not a fix.

---

## Idea_01 — SupportAudit (B-0121)

| Attack | Design change |
|---|---|
| "A wrong support is out-of-distribution; you are measuring OOD robustness, not support reliance." | Wrong supports drawn from **the same benchmark's own support pool**, differing only in class. Never synthetic, never corrupted. |
| "Retention has no floor — 0.4 could be good or bad." | **Two floors**, both measured: a support-free saliency baseline and a random-mask baseline. Retention is reported against them, never alone. |
| "Wrong-support lists are a researcher degree of freedom." | Lists generated by a **frozen seeded procedure, committed before any method runs**, and released. |
| "Only tests old methods." | **SAM3 arm** promoted into the core matrix, not left as future work. |
| "τ = 0.2 was chosen to make the result work." | τ frozen at pre-registration; sensitivity sweep reported alongside. |
| "One benchmark's degeneracy is not the field's." | Stratification is the primary axis, not a subgroup analysis. `S1` vs `S3` is the headline structure. |

**Answered in prose only:** "this is an evaluation paper, not a method paper." The response is that
the object of study is deliberately the evaluation, and all three outcomes are different useful
papers. A reviewer may still not want that paper.

## Idea_02 — MemorySurgeon (A-0017)

| Attack | Design change |
|---|---|
| **A1: "Reversibility correlates with sequence difficulty, not with anything about memory."** | **Partial correlation** controlling for no-op recovery, failure count and distractor density, over a **comparator set frozen before any comparator runs**, scored by its **minimum**. The prototype makes the stakes concrete: naive `r` of 0.86 fell to 0.03 on one comparator. |
| "Replay is not deterministic; your arm differences are harness noise." | **E0 gate.** The `no_op` arm must reproduce the original failure bit-for-bit or the project stops. `check_replay_determinism` raises rather than warns. |
| "You cherry-picked recoverable failures." | Fixed harvest protocol, **zero curation**; the ambiguous-reappearance stratum is reported separately and never dropped. |
| "Deleting entries changes recency encodings; that is your effect." | `slot_control` arm isolates slot occupancy from content. |
| "Corpus size is unknown and may be tiny." | Yield reported at E1 **before** any claim, with an explicit descope path. |
| "SAM2 is a moving target." | SAM3 arm funded (E7). |

**Found during implementation, not by a reviewer** — and both are more serious than anything on the
list above:

- **The sham arm was specified backwards.** Stage 6 asked for "delete post-error entries but re-insert
  count-matched pre-error duplicates." That arm still removes the corruption, so it performs the
  therapeutic action and would match `targeted` even when the instrument is perfectly valid. `P3` was
  unpassable by construction. Redefined as a vacuous control; the literal version survives as
  `slot_control`.
- **Pooling long failures dilutes the H2 test toward zero.** `targeted` and `reset` are the same
  operation once FIFO has flushed all pre-error entries, so those failures contribute an exact zero
  to the gap. In the prototype a genuine +0.130 subset gap pooled to +0.040 — a failure of the
  pre-registered bar in the world where the decomposition is real. P2 is now read on the H2-testable
  subset, whose share is reported at E1 before any P2 claim.

## Idea_03 — RehearsalGrid (C-0090)

| Attack | Design change |
|---|---|
| **"Your control arm contradicts your premise."** LLaVA-1.5's mix already contains text-only ShareGPT, so "reproduce the published recipe" and "`A0` = no rehearsal" cannot both hold. **Fatal as specified.** | **Text-stripped base with token-budget re-matching** across all arms; the published-recipe run demoted to a reference point. The claim narrows to "a controlled variant of the recipe", and §3 says so. |
| "Two of your four strata have unusable denominators." | **Absolute deltas co-primary** with retention ratios; **denominator-adequacy gate applied before the arms**, with failing strata replaced rather than explained away. |
| "'If the conventions diverge, that's the finding' is unfalsifiable-adjacent." | **Per-layer matching declared the pre-registered primary**, justified by documented per-layer gradient disparity. Global-norm demoted to a sensitivity analysis. |
| "Your 80% rule is a ratio of differences with a small denominator." | **MDE declared before the grid runs**; shares left uninterpreted below it; bootstrap CIs on every share. |
| "One model family, one scale." | **Cross-family replication promoted into the core design** (3 runs on prismatic-vlms). Does not fully answer the attack, and §19 says so. |
| "Robins 1995 predicted this." | Reframed: the refutation branch is the stronger paper. `KNOWN FACT` unresolvable before data. |

## The revival: Idea_02's method arm became mandatory

The meta-auditor's bias audit found the locked Top 5 was **measurement-tilted**, and issued a
mandatory clause rather than a recommendation.

**A-0017's method arm was elevated from optional secondary analysis to a required deliverable.** If
targeted deletion beats a full reset by the pre-registered ≥10-point margin, the paper *must* ship
the surgery as a training-free SAM2 wrapper benchmarked head-to-head against SAMURAI and DAM4SAM.
The audit was explicit that this is **a real scope change, not relabeling: the Research Package must
budget the head-to-head comparison runs.** It is now E9 (~40 GPU-h, conditional on P2) and it is why
Idea_02 is not a pure measurement paper.

The reasoning: A-0017 is the only Top-5 idea that can graduate from measurement to method inside one
budget, converting an "upper-bound diagnostic" into a candidate tracker improvement.

**C-0028 was revived to *funded first alternate*.** Its zero-GPU offline dry-run gate (H-arm
constructibility: KS < 0.05 similarity matching plus noun-rate checks on 10K generated batches) runs
immediately, so a fully de-risked training-recipe flagship sits on the shelf if C-0090 dies at its
power gate. It was deliberately **not** co-picked with C-0090 — two VLM-training studies sharing eval
infrastructure would recreate monoculture in the opposite direction.

## The pre-committed fallback ladder

Agreed in advance so that a failure does not reopen the selection debate:

1. **C-0090 dies at its power gate** → promote **C-0028** (offline gate already passed by then; the
   decision triple alone, 9 runs ≈ 3,600 A100-h, settles it).
2. **B-0121 proves infeasible** (benchmark degeneracy so total that even the certification branch is
   vacuous) → promote **M5** if its $0 desk survey found ≥15 auditable pairs; else **B-0212** off its
   passed MVE.
3. **A-0017 is scooped before the margin test runs** → promote **B-0212** off its passed MVE;
   **A-0002** (same cluster, ~30 GPU-h killer battery, cleanest causal design in Cluster A) is the
   cheap stopgap if B-0212's MVE failed.

## The disagreement nobody resolved

**Should Idea_03 be in this round at all?** It is a language-retention study in a portfolio otherwise
built on segmentation and tracking, and the same 2,450 A100-h would buy the C-0028 decision triple.

The panel's position, which prevailed: C-0090 is the **deliberate expensive pick and the direct
counterweight to the funnel's documented cheapness bias** — the only Top-5 idea whose every outcome
branch changes how people *train*. It was chosen over C-0028 for the single training slot on being
2.2× cheaper, having zero feasibility gates left, and having been ranked #1 on ROI by its own
cluster.

**This was a portfolio judgement, not a scientific one, and no experiment settles it.** Recorded
rather than smoothed over, because it is the most likely thing to be second-guessed later.
